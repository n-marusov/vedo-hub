= Quick Start
:description: How to access VEDO Core and start working with ontologies.

== Accessing the Platform

Once the VEDO Core stack is running (see the link:../../developer-guide/modules/ROOT/pages/getting-started.adoc[Developer Guide — Getting Started] for setup), open the frontend in your browser:

[source]
----
http://localhost:3000
----

== Logging In

Authentication is handled via Keycloak. After navigating to the frontend, you will be redirected to the login page.

Supported identity providers:

* ESIA / Gosuslugi
* Yandex
* VK
* Google

== Creating Groups

Groups organize your projects into teams, departments, or domains. Groups can be nested (subgroups) up to 5 levels deep.

To create a group:

. Navigate to *Groups* from the sidebar.
. Click *New group* — you will be taken to the create group page (`/dashboard/groups/new`).
. Enter a *Group name* (required) — a URL-friendly slug is auto-generated from the name.
. Choose a *Visibility level*:
  * *Private* — only members can view the group and its projects.
  * *Internal* — any authenticated user (except external) can view.
  * *Public* — anyone can view without authentication.
. Click *Create group*.

After creation:

* A success Toast notification appears at the bottom-right.
* You are redirected to the group detail page (`/dashboard/groups/:id`).

=== Creating Subgroups

To create a group inside another group (subgroup):

. Navigate to the parent group page.
. Click *New group* (or navigate to `/dashboard/groups/new?parent_id=<uuid>`).
. The visibility is inherited from the parent group (read-only on the create page).
. A subgroup cannot be more visible than its parent (e.g., a *Public* subgroup under a *Private* parent is rejected).

== Creating Your First Ontology

. Click *New Ontology* on the dashboard.
. Enter a name and description.
. Click *Create* — you will be taken to the ontology editor.

== Importing an Existing Ontology

VEDO Core supports importing from the following formats:

* Turtle (`.ttl`)
* RDF/XML (`.rdf`, `.owl`)
* OWL (`.owl`)

To import:

. Open the ontology list.
. Click *Import*.
. Select the file and format.
. Click *Upload*.
